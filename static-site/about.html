About

A focused team with a long view.

Henrry Martinez and Jeremy Perez founded HJ4 Capital to acquire, improve, and manage income-producing real estate with a long-term focus.

Our story

Founded by Henrry and Jeremy.

HJ4 Capital focuses on multifamily and other income-producing properties in select Southeast and Sun Belt markets. Henrry and Jeremy evaluate each opportunity based on local demand, property condition, and operating potential.

The founders review comparable rents, expenses, taxes, insurance, and capital needs together. They share acquisition decisions and remain involved in property operations after closing.
